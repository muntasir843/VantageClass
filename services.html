Good to know

Questions wehear often.

Is pricing really fixed?

Yes. Every quote is all-in — tolls, gratuity and any airport meet-and-greet fees are included before you confirm. The price you’re quoted is the price you pay, with no surge and no surcharges added afterward.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We track your flight number directly, so your pickup time adjusts automatically — including diversions and early arrivals. You don’t need to call and update us, and delays don’t trigger extra charges.

How far in advance should I book?

Dispatch is open 24/7 and we can often accommodate same-day requests. For weddings, multi-day shuttles or large corporate moves, booking one to two weeks ahead gives us the most flexibility to match vehicles to your exact headcount.

Can I change my pickup or drop-off after booking?

Yes — text or call dispatch and we’ll adjust the route. For hourly and as-directed bookings, additional stops are already part of the service. For point-to-point transfers, a revised address may adjust the fare, which we’ll confirm with you first.

Do you offer corporate billing?

Yes. Corporate accounts get a dedicated coordinator, consolidated monthly invoicing, and net-30 terms on request. Reach out through the business & events section above to get set up.
